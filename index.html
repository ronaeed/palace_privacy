Palace privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Anora Games

Palace is a card game you play on your own device against computer players. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app stores

To remember your game between sessions, Palace saves the following only on your device:

  • your settings (such as hints, vibration and bot speed), the name you type in and the animal avatar you pick;
  • the round in progress, so you can continue it later;
  • how many times each player has lost this session.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No collection of personal information, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No network requests: the game works fully offline.

Permissions

The app may use your phone's vibration motor for a short buzz when it is your turn. You can turn this off in Settings. It does not request access to storage, the camera, the microphone or location.

Children

Palace is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
